Licence: GNU General Public License v3 or later.

Modifications relative to uBlock Origin Lite (GPLv3 section 5(a)); the build
checks them file by file (`scripts/verify-stage.mjs`):

- new toolbar popup and dashboard pages (`popup.html`, `dashboard.html` and the
  `ui/` folder); upstream's own pages are kept as `popup-classic.html` and
  `dashboard-classic.html` and used as a fallback and for embedded editors;
- a generated `css/default.css` that re-colours upstream's remaining pages
  (picker, zapper, report and others) through their CSS variables;
- the extension name: `short_name` in `manifest.json` and `extName` in all
  `_locales/*/messages.json` say "Blocker"; on Firefox also the manifest
  `name` (set to the localized name) and the add-on id
  (`browser_specific_settings.gecko.id`);
- optionally, icons in `img/` replaced from `assets/icons/extension/`
  (identical to upstream's in this repository);
- upstream's `log.txt` is left out of the package;
- for Safari, a host app (macOS app window, iOS setup screen) generated with
  Apple's `safari-web-extension-converter`.

The background code, content scripts and rulesets, and every other part of
the manifest, are those of uBlock Origin Lite.

"uBlock Origin" and "uBlock Origin Lite" are the names of the upstream
projects. Blocker is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.

## Filter lists

The rulesets inside the extension are converted at build time from public
filter lists (for example uBlock filters, EasyList, EasyPrivacy, Peter Lowe's
list and many regional lists; the full list with home pages is
`upstream/platform/mv3/rulesets.json` and the Filter lists pane). Each filter
list remains under its own licence and belongs to its maintainers; see the
list's home page. Examples: the uBlock filters (uAssets) are GPLv3; EasyList
and EasyPrivacy are dual-licensed under GPLv3 and CC BY-SA 3.0.
